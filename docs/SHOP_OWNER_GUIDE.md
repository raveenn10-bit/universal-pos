# Harsh Apex Universal POS - Shop Owner & Administrator Guide

## 1. First-Time Setup Wizard
When launching Harsh Apex Universal POS for the first time:
1. **Create Owner Account**:
   - Enter your administrator username and full name.
   - Set a strong password (minimum 8 characters).
   - Optionally configure a 4-digit quick Cashier PIN for rapid shift unlocks.
2. **Select Initial Business Profile**:
   - Choose your business type (General Retail, Supermarket, Mobile Store, Electronics, Shoes, Fashion).
   - Alternatively, import your developer-signed `.apexlicense` package.
3. **Configure Store Branding**:
   - Business name, address, telephone numbers, and receipt footer message.

---

## 2. Fast Checkout (Cashier POS)
- Press **F1** anywhere in the application to open the Fast Checkout terminal.
- **Scanning Items**: Scan item barcode with your optical scanner or type the SKU/Barcode and press `Enter`.
- **Keyboard Controls**:
  - `+` / `-` buttons to adjust cart quantities.
  - Line discounts and cart discounts.
- **Customer Lookup**: Select an existing customer or click `Quick-Add Customer` to register a customer on the fly without abandoning the active cart.
- **Split Tender Payments**:
  - Click `Proceed to Payment` (or press `F12`).
  - Enter tender amounts across Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, or Customer Store Credit.
  - Review change to return.
  - Click `Confirm Sale`.
- **Printing & PDF**:
  - Save or print 80mm/58mm thermal receipts with verification QR code.
  - Save or print official A4 tax invoices with multi-page pagination.

---

## 3. Inventory & Specialized Workflows
- **Supermarkets**: Manage batch numbers and expiration dates with FIFO alerts.
- **Mobile Stores**: Register device IMEI 1 and IMEI 2, condition (New, Used, Refurbished), and warranty durations.
- **Shoes & Fashion**: Manage size and color variant grids under a parent SKU.
- **Reasoned Adjustments**: Add or remove stock with mandatory audit reasons.
